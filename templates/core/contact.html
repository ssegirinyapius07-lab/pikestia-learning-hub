Have a question, correction, suggestion, partnership idea, content concern, or technical problem? Use the information below to contact Pikestia Learning Hub.

General support

For general questions about the platform, accounts, learning resources, navigation, or technical problems, email support@pikestia.com.

Copyright concerns

For copyright or content-rights concerns, email copyright@pikestia.com. Include the relevant page or material and enough information for the issue to be investigated.

Before you contact us

For a technical problem, it helps to include the page you were using, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and the browser or device you were using. Never include your password, authentication codes, recovery tokens, or other account secrets in a support message.

Content corrections

If you find an incorrect explanation, broken link, outdated opportunity, spelling error, or other content problem, tell us the page and describe the correction. For time-sensitive opportunities, also provide the original source where possible.

Partnerships and contributions

If you represent a student organization, university community, educational project, or other relevant initiative and want to discuss a legitimate collaboration, contact the team with a clear description of the proposal and the organization involved.

Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This policy explains the behaviour expected from everyone using Pikestia Learning Hub. The goal is to keep the platform useful, respectful, secure, and suitable for university-focused learning.

1. Use the platform lawfully

You must use Pikestia in accordance with applicable law, institutional rules, and these policies. Do not use the platform to plan, facilitate, or distribute material connected with unlawful activity.

2. Respect other people

Do not use Pikestia to threaten, harass, intimidate, stalk, impersonate, discriminate against, or deliberately target another person. Academic disagreement is acceptable; abusive conduct is not.

3. No spam or deceptive activity

Do not send unsolicited bulk messages, create misleading submissions, manipulate engagement systems, distribute deceptive links, or use automated activity to interfere with normal platform use.

4. Protect account security

Do not attempt to obtain another person's password, session, account, recovery token, or other private authentication information. Do not share stolen credentials or knowingly use an account that you are not authorized to access.

5. Do not bypass security

Do not attempt to bypass authentication, authorization, rate limits, access controls, content restrictions, or other security mechanisms. Do not probe or attack the platform in a way that could disrupt service or expose information belonging to other users.

6. Do not upload harmful code or files

Where uploads are supported, do not submit malware, destructive scripts, credential-stealing code, malicious files, or content designed to compromise another person's device or account.

7. Respect intellectual property

Do not upload or publish copyrighted, trademarked, confidential, or otherwise protected material unless you have the necessary rights or permission. Do not present another person's work as your own.

8. Protect private information

Do not publish another person's private information without a lawful and appropriate reason. Avoid placing passwords, financial credentials, identity documents, private addresses, or other sensitive information into public learning or contact forms.

9. Academic integrity

Pikestia exists to support learning. Do not use the platform to facilitate plagiarism, impersonation, cheating, unauthorized examination assistance, or other academic misconduct. Use educational resources to understand and develop your own knowledge.

10. Do not manipulate platform content

Do not deliberately alter, corrupt, scrape, flood, or manipulate content or functionality in a way that harms the accuracy, availability, or experience of the platform or other users.

11. Responsible automation

Automated access must not create unreasonable load, bypass access controls, evade rate limits, or interfere with normal service. Respect published restrictions and use reasonable request rates.

12. Reporting problems

If you discover a security issue, harmful content, copyright concern, or serious abuse, report it through the appropriate channel rather than exploiting or spreading the problem. The Contact page provides a general route for support.

13. Enforcement

Violations may lead to content removal, access restrictions, account suspension, or other appropriate action depending on the nature and seriousness of the issue. Security incidents may also be investigated and recorded for operational or legal purposes.

14. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as Pikestia adds features or responds to new security, community, and educational needs. The latest published version applies to use of the platform.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Pikestia Learning Hub uses cookies and similar browser storage technologies to provide a secure, usable, and consistent experience.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small piece of information stored by your browser for a website. Cookies can help a website remember a session, security state, preference, or consent choice.

2. Essential cookies

Some cookies are necessary for the platform to function. Depending on the features you use, essential cookies may support:

  • user authentication and login sessions;
  • security protections and request validation;
  • cookie-consent preferences;
  • interface preferences such as display mode;
  • basic operation of protected areas.

Because these technologies support core functionality or security, disabling them may prevent parts of Pikestia from working correctly.

3. Optional cookies and analytics

Optional analytics or similar technologies, where enabled, should be used only in accordance with the consent choices presented to you and applicable requirements. Optional technologies are intended to help understand general usage and improve the platform rather than to replace essential security mechanisms.

4. What we use cookies for

Cookie and session information may help Pikestia:

  • keep you signed in when you choose to use authenticated features;
  • remember settings between visits;
  • protect forms and sessions against misuse;
  • remember whether you have made a cookie-consent choice;
  • support reliable navigation and platform functionality.

5. Cookie consent

When Pikestia presents a cookie-consent banner, you may be offered choices such as accepting optional cookies, rejecting optional cookies, or managing your preferences. Your choice is used to determine which optional technologies may be enabled.

6. Browser controls

Most modern browsers allow you to view, delete, block, or restrict cookies. Browser settings vary, and blocking all cookies can affect authentication, security, preferences, and other functionality.

7. Sessions and local storage

Not every browser-side technology is a cookie. A website may also use session mechanisms or local browser storage to remember information. Pikestia uses these mechanisms only as needed for supported features and user experience.

8. Third-party services

If Pikestia integrates an external service, that service may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Such technologies are governed by the external provider's policies and settings. External links should be reviewed before you provide information or enable optional tracking on another site.

9. Security

Cookies used for authentication or security are treated as part of the platform's security boundary. Do not share session information or allow other people to use an authenticated browser session on a device you do not trust.

10. Changes to this policy

Cookie practices may change as Pikestia adds or removes features. When material changes are made, this page will be updated and the date at the top will be revised.

11. Questions

If you have questions about cookies or your consent choices, visit the Contact page.

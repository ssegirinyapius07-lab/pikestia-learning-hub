Copyright Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Pikestia Learning Hub respects the rights of authors, educators, publishers, universities, organizations, and other content owners. This policy explains the basic copyright standards for material published or referenced on the platform.

1. Original material

Original Pikestia material should be created by the platform or by contributors who have the appropriate rights to provide it. Contributors should not copy protected work and present it as original Pikestia content.

2. Licensed and permitted material

Material may be used where the necessary permission, license, public-domain status, or other lawful basis allows its use. Where attribution or license conditions apply, they should be respected.

3. Third-party educational material

Educational value does not automatically remove copyright restrictions. A textbook page, article, photograph, diagram, video, lecture note, or other work should not be uploaded or republished merely because it is useful to students.

4. Attribution

Where a source is used under a license or permission that requires attribution, the appropriate attribution should be included. Links to the original source may also help users identify the origin of the material.

5. User submissions

If Pikestia provides a feature for users to submit learning material, the submitting user is responsible for ensuring that the submission does not violate another person's rights. Do not upload complete copyrighted books, paid course materials, examination papers, or other protected resources without appropriate permission.

6. Copyright concerns

If you believe material on Pikestia infringes your copyright or another protected right, please provide enough information for the issue to be investigated. Useful information can include:

  • your name and a reliable contact method;
  • identification of the copyrighted work or protected material;
  • the URL or location of the material on Pikestia;
  • an explanation of why you believe the material is infringing;
  • information showing that you own the rights or are authorized to act for the rights holder.

7. Reporting address

Copyright concerns may be sent to copyright@pikestia.com. Do not send passwords, financial credentials, or unrelated private information with a copyright report.

8. Review and response

Reported material may be reviewed to determine what action is appropriate. Depending on the circumstances, action may include requesting clarification, correcting attribution, restricting access, removing material, or contacting the relevant contributor.

9. Counterclaims and disputes

If material was removed or restricted because of a copyright complaint and you believe you have the necessary rights or permission, provide relevant supporting information through the available contact channel so the issue can be reviewed.

10. Trademarks and branding

Copyright and trademark rights are different forms of intellectual-property protection. Do not use Pikestia branding, logos, or other protected marks in a way that falsely suggests sponsorship, partnership, or official endorsement.

11. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as Pikestia develops its content-management and reporting processes. The current version will be published on this page.

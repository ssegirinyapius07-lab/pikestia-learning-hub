Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Pikestia Learning Hub is a university-focused learning and knowledge platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Pikestia, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

We aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide and improve the platform. The information we may handle depends on how you use Pikestia.

  • Account information: If you create an account, we may store information such as your email address and account credentials needed to provide authentication and account security.
  • Preferences: Your display preference, such as light or dark mode, may be stored so the interface can remember your choice.
  • Saved learning activity: Features such as bookmarks may store references to learning resources that you choose to save.
  • Content you submit: If the platform provides forms or publishing features that allow you to submit information, the information you deliberately provide may be stored and processed for that feature.
  • Technical information: Normal web requests may contain technical information such as browser, device, IP address, request time, and error information. Such information may be used for security, troubleshooting, and reliable operation.

2. Anonymous browsing

You can browse public educational material without creating an account where the feature is available. An account is generally needed for features that must be associated with a particular user, such as bookmarks, account preferences, or protected areas.

3. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • create and maintain user accounts;
  • authenticate users and protect account security;
  • remember settings and preferences;
  • provide saved-resource and learning features;
  • respond to support or contact requests;
  • detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
  • diagnose technical problems and improve reliability;
  • understand how platform features are used where appropriate and permitted.

4. Information we do not need

Pikestia is designed around educational use. We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information merely to browse ordinary public learning resources. Please do not submit passwords, financial account credentials, identity documents, medical records, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary contact or content forms unless a feature explicitly requires it and provides appropriate instructions.

5. Account security

Account information is protected using reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the platform. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for using a strong password. If you believe your account has been accessed without permission, contact Pikestia through the available support channel as soon as possible.

6. Cookies and sessions

Pikestia may use cookies and server-side sessions for authentication, security, preferences, and core functionality. Some optional technologies may require consent where applicable. See the Cookie Policy for more information.

7. Sharing of information

We do not treat your personal information as public simply because you use Pikestia. Information may be accessed by authorized people who need it to operate, secure, maintain, or support the platform. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the platform, its users, or other people.

8. Educational content

Learning resources, news, opportunities, and other public material may be visible to other visitors. Do not place private personal information inside material intended for public publication.

9. Third-party services and links

Pikestia may link to external websites, services, institutions, scholarship providers, universities, publishers, or other resources. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not by this policy. Review the privacy information of an external service before submitting personal information to it.

10. Data retention

We aim to retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate operational needs, security, dispute handling, or other applicable requirements. Retention periods can differ depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

11. Your choices

Depending on the feature and applicable requirements, you may be able to update account information, change display preferences, remove saved items, manage cookie choices, or contact us about information associated with your account.

12. Children's and student use

Pikestia is intended primarily for university-focused learning and student communities. Users should provide only information they are permitted to share and should follow the rules of their institution and applicable law.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the platform, its features, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

14. Contact

If you have a privacy question, request, or concern, use the Contact page. For account-security concerns, provide enough information for us to understand the issue without sending passwords or other secrets.
